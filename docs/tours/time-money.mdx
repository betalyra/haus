You're selling seats for a workshop. Time and money are where this kind of data quietly goes wrong: a meeting an hour off after a clock change, a price that drifts to `19.899999`.

<Story types>

<Step label="workshop.haus" code={steps[0]}>

### Which 10:00?

Which time zone? Is `19.9` exact? Is the currency part of the price? To haus, these are just a `String` and a number.

</Step>

<Step label="workshop.haus" code={steps[1]}>

### A meeting happens in a place

It starts at 10:00 **in Lisbon**. `ZonedDateTime` keeps the local time, the offset and the zone, summer time included.

</Step>

<Step label="workshop.haus" code={steps[2]}>

### How long, and until when?

It lasts a `Duration`. Sign-up closes on a `Date`: a day, with no time or zone to get wrong.

</Step>

<Step label="workshop.haus" code={steps[3]}>

### Don't let 19.90 become 19.9

`Money(19.90, EUR)` keeps **every digit** and its currency. haus never rounds a number through a float.

</Step>

<Step label="workshop.haus" code={steps[4]}>

### When exactly was it booked?

A booking happened at one moment, wherever you look from: an `Instant`, in UTC. Any currency works, at any precision.

</Step>

<Step lang="haus-schema" label="workshop.schema.haus" code={steps[5]}>

### Checked, not trusted

Time types come from `std/time`, money from `@haus/money`. With a schema, `Date("2027-02-30")` is **an error**, not a date.

</Step>

</Story>

## Which time type?

| Type | For | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `Date` | a day: birthdays, deadlines | `Date("2027-03-15")` |
| `Time` | a time of day: opening hours | `Time("09:00:00")` |
| `DateTime` | a date and time with no zone | `DateTime("2027-03-29T10:00:00")` |
| `Instant` | a moment: logs, timestamps | `Instant("2027-03-01T08:15:30Z")` |
| `ZonedDateTime` | a time in a place: meetings | `ZonedDateTime("…+01:00[Europe/Lisbon]")` |
| `Duration` | a length of time | `Duration("PT1H30M")` |
| `YearMonth` | a month: billing periods | `YearMonth("2027-03")` |
| `TimeZone` | a zone on its own | `TimeZone("Europe/Lisbon")` |

The exact formats are in the [std/time profile](/docs/spec/time).
